* The player screen scrolls, so the speed and sleep-timer controls are
  reachable on smaller watches (they were off-screen)
* A "now playing" row in the library takes you back to the player
* The uploader says when it lacks local-network permission instead of showing
  an address nothing can reach, and starts as soon as permission is granted
* Chapters are read from the file rather than from playback, so they appear for
  a book resumed at a saved position, and large books parse in a fraction of a
  second
