Aloud is an open-source, standalone audiobook player for Wear OS watches.
It is an offline audiobook app for Pixel Watch users who want to listen to their
own DRM-free MP3 and M4B files without carrying a phone.

Copy books to the watch through Aloud's built-in browser uploader over local
Wi-Fi. The uploader is protected by a six-digit PIN and needs no cable,
companion app, account, cloud service, or internet connection. Once a book is
stored on the watch, connect Bluetooth headphones and listen anywhere.

Features

* Standalone local MP3 and M4B playback on Wear OS
* Per-book resume position across app restarts
* Chapter navigation for M4B Nero and QuickTime chapters and MP3 ID3 CHAP frames
* Playback speed from 0.75x to 2.0x
* Persistent 15, 30, 60, and 120-minute sleep timer
* Background playback with the screen off
* System media controls and Bluetooth headset buttons
* Recovery from temporary Bluetooth disconnection
* Browser-based local Wi-Fi uploader with PIN protection
* No account, cloud sync, analytics, tracking, or third-party services

Aloud plays only the files you put on it. It is intended for your own DRM-free
audiobook library: running, walking, travel, and sleep. It does not sell,
stream, or download books, and it cannot play DRM-protected files.

Requires a Wear OS 3 or newer watch with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth headphones. The
Wi-Fi uploader works on a local network only; Aloud sends nothing to the
internet.
