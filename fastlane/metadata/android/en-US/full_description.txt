Aloud is a no-frills, open-source audiobook player that runs entirely on your
watch. It plays your own DRM-free MP3 and M4B files over Bluetooth, remembers
where you stopped in every book, follows chapters, and never needs a phone.

Getting books onto the watch needs no cable and no companion app: Aloud serves a
small upload page over your own WiFi, protected by a 6-digit PIN shown on the
watch. Open it in any browser on the same network and drag your files in.

Features

* Local MP3 and M4B playback, standalone on the watch
* Resume exactly where you left off, per book, across restarts
* Chapter navigation: Nero and QuickTime chapters in M4B, ID3 CHAP in MP3
* Playback speed 0.75-2.0x, remembered between sessions
* Sleep timer (15/30/60/120 min) that survives a restart
* Keeps playing with the screen off; system media controls and Bluetooth
  buttons work
* Built-in PIN-protected upload server; no account, no cloud, no tracking

Aloud has no network access beyond the upload server you start yourself, no
analytics and no third-party services. Your library never leaves your watch.

Built for the Pixel Watch. Requires Wear OS 4 or newer.
